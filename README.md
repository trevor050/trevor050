I'm Trevor. I've been writing software since before high school. These days I sit closer to product and growth than pure engineering, mostly because building the thing stopped being the hard part.

"ULM" is the name I put on personal work. You'll see it on a few repos below.

## What I'm doing now

**[HonorlyAI](https://honorlyai.com)**, co-founder and CTO. A K-12 AI agent platform. I do all of the engineering. We launched in April 2026. The governance and classroom-policy material we give away to schools lives in [honorlyai-resources](https://github.com/trevor050/honorlyai-resources).

**[Aeneas Technologies](https://aeneastech.com)**, Head of Growth. A K-12 cybersecurity firm. I built the in-house software side: AenSignal, which does domain and exposure intelligence for school districts, and [ULMcode](https://github.com/trevor050/ulmcode), an agentic penetration-testing harness built on OpenCode.

**OpenAI Student Collective**, Campus Lead at Rutgers, inaugural class.

## Things I've shipped

**[MyTaxReceipt](https://mytaxreceipt.org)** turns your federal tax bill into an itemized receipt, then helps you write to Congress about the lines you'd change. I built it solo. It got recommended by [Recomendo](https://archive.recomendo.com/posts/where-your-tax-dollars-actually-go) and [Idea Surplus Disorder](https://www.ideasurplusdisorder.com/122/), among others. [Source](https://github.com/trevor050/myTaxReciept).

**[motor-wrapper](https://github.com/trevor050/motor-wrapper)** is a C++17 wrapper for Nanotec motor controllers, written for a robotics team, with a test suite that runs against a simulated drive so you don't need the hardware on your desk.
